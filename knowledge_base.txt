ORDERING

Customers can ask about available products, prices, sizes, colours and designs before placing an order.

Customers should provide the necessary product details when placing an order.

PAYMENT

Customers should confirm the total cost of their order before making payment.

Customers should keep their payment receipt or proof of payment after completing a transaction.

DELIVERY

Delivery charges depend on the customer's location and the delivery method.

The customer should confirm the delivery fee before completing an order.

Customers should provide accurate delivery information to avoid delays.

RETURNS AND EXCHANGES

Customers should contact Wilfreda's Collection as soon as possible if there is a problem with an order.

Returns or exchanges depend on the condition of the item and the specific circumstances of the order.

Customers should not return an item without first contacting customer support.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

The AI customer support agent should be polite, helpful and professional.

The AI agent should answer questions using the information contained in this knowledge base.

If the answer is not available in the knowledge base, the AI agent should not invent information. It should politely tell the customer that the information is not currently available and recommend contacting Wilfreda's Collection directly.

The AI agent should maintain the context of the conversation and use previous messages when answering follow-up questions.

The AI agent should never claim that an item is available if availability has not been confirmed.

The AI agent should never invent prices, delivery times or company policies.

TONE OF SERVICE

Customer support should be friendly, respectful, professional and easy to understand.

The goal is to help customers find products, understand prices and policies, place orders and resolve basic questions.
